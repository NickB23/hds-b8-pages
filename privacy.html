Privacy Policy

This policy covers the "HDS B8 Gmail Fallback" Google API client, a personal automation built and operated by a single individual for their own home-network monitoring system. It is not a public product, has no other users, and is not distributed to anyone else.

What it does

The client sends one automated email, via the Gmail API's https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send scope, when a monitoring node in the operator's own home-network stack loses contact with another node it is watching, and again when contact recovers. That is its only function.

Data accessed

The client holds only the gmail.send scope. It cannot read, search, list, or delete any email in the connected mailbox, and it does not access any other Google data (contacts, calendar, drive, etc.). The content of the emails it sends is limited to a timestamp and the name of the monitoring node involved — no personal, financial, or third-party data of any kind.

Data storage and retention

No email content or Google account data is stored by this client beyond what is needed to authenticate the single send operation. OAuth credentials are stored locally, encrypted at rest, on hardware the operator personally controls, and are never transmitted to any third party.

Data sharing

No data is shared with any third party. There is no advertising, analytics, or other integration.
